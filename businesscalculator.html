Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about financial ratios and formulas.

What is the difference between Profit Margin and Markup?
Profit Margin is calculated as profit divided by revenue (selling price). Markup is profit divided by cost. Margin shows what percentage of sales is profit, while markup shows how much you added to the cost price.
How is Break-Even Point calculated?
Break-Even Point (Units) = Fixed Costs / (Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit). It tells you how many products you must sell to cover all your costs without making a loss or profit.
